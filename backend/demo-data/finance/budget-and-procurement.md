# Budget and Procurement Guidelines (Demo/Fictional)

All departmental purchases above a threshold value require a purchase requisition approved by the Department Admin and routed through the Finance Department for budget availability check.

Procurement categories:
- Below Rs. 25,000: Direct purchase with 3 quotations
- Rs. 25,000 to Rs. 2,00,000: Limited tender, minimum 3 vendors
- Above Rs. 2,00,000: Open tender via the e-procurement portal

Budget utilization reports are submitted quarterly by each department to Finance for consolidation. Unspent budget lapses at the end of the financial year unless a carry-forward request is approved.

Emergency procurement (e.g., critical IT hardware failure) may bypass standard tendering with post-facto approval from the Department Admin within 7 days.
