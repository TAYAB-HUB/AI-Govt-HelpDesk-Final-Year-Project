# Pension Commutation and Withdrawal Rules (Demo/Fictional)

Retiring employees may opt to commute (convert to a lump sum) up to 40% of their monthly pension. The commuted amount is calculated using the commutation table applicable at the age next birthday.

Commutation application must be submitted along with the pension application (Form P-1) or within one year of retirement to avoid a medical examination requirement.

Provident Fund withdrawal (for applicable schemes) is processed separately from pension and typically settled within 30 days of the final application, provided all department clearances are complete.

Partial withdrawal from the General Provident Fund for specific purposes (medical treatment, education, housing) is permitted subject to eligibility rules and requires Department Admin countersignature.
