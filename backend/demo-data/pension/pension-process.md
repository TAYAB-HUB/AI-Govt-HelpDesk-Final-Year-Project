# Pension Process Guide (Demo/Fictional)

Retiring employees must submit Form P-1 (Pension Application) at least 8 months before the date of retirement, along with service verification records.

Pension processing stages:
1. Service verification by HR (2 months before retirement)
2. Calculation of qualifying service and average emoluments by the Pension Cell
3. Draft Pension Payment Order (PPO) review
4. Final PPO issuance and handover on the date of retirement

Family pension is granted to the spouse or eligible dependents in case of the employee's death, at 50% of the last drawn pension for the first 7 years or up to age 67, whichever is earlier, and 30% thereafter.

Gratuity is paid separately, calculated as per qualifying service and last drawn basic pay, subject to a statutory ceiling.
