# IT Asset and Device Request FAQ (Demo/Fictional)

Q: How do I request a new laptop or peripheral?
A: Submit an asset request ticket under "IT - Asset Request" specifying business justification; Department Admin approval is required before procurement.

Q: What is the laptop refresh cycle?
A: Standard laptops are refreshed every 4 years, or earlier in case of irreparable hardware failure verified by the IT team.

Q: How do I return an asset when I transfer or resign?
A: Complete the Asset Return Form and get it signed by the IT helpdesk; clearance is required before final settlement or relieving order.

Q: Can I use personal USB drives on office computers?
A: External storage devices are restricted by default; exceptions require Department Admin approval and a security scan.
