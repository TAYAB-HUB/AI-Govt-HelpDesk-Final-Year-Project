# Official Travel and Vehicle Policy (Demo/Fictional)

Official vehicle requisition for local duty travel must be submitted at least 1 day in advance through the Administration ticket category, specifying purpose, destination, and timing.

Outstation official travel requires a Tour Approval Order signed by the Department Admin before booking tickets or accommodation.

Vehicle usage priority: emergency/VIP movement, official inspections, then routine departmental work, in that order, when demand exceeds available vehicles.

Fuel and maintenance expenses for official vehicles are tracked by the Administration department and reconciled monthly with the Finance department's budget records.
