# Performance Appraisal Guidelines (Demo/Fictional)

Annual Performance Appraisal Reports (APAR) are filled between April and June for the previous financial year. Every employee must complete the self-assessment section within 15 days of the portal opening.

Appraisal grading scale: Outstanding, Very Good, Good, Average, Below Average. A minimum of "Good" is required in the last 5 years for promotion eligibility.

Grievances regarding appraisal scores can be raised with the Department Admin within 30 days of score publication. A review committee will respond within 45 days.

Training completion, attendance record, and any commendations or disciplinary actions are considered as supporting inputs during appraisal.
