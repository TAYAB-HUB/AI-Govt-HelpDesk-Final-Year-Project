# Leave Policy (Demo/Fictional)

All permanent employees are entitled to 30 days of Earned Leave (EL), 12 days of Casual Leave (CL), and 15 days of Half-Pay Leave (HPL) per calendar year, credited on 1 January and 1 July.

To apply for leave, submit the Leave Application Form through the internal portal at least 3 working days in advance for planned leave. Casual Leave requests of 1-2 days may be submitted to your reporting officer directly.

Leave approval authority:
- Up to 5 days: Reporting Officer
- 6 to 15 days: Department Officer
- More than 15 days: Department Admin

Unused Earned Leave can be carried forward up to a maximum accumulation of 300 days, subject to encashment rules at the time of retirement or resignation.

Maternity leave is granted for 180 days for the first two children. Paternity leave is granted for 15 days, to be availed within 6 months of the child's birth.

Leave without pay may be granted in exceptional circumstances at the discretion of the Department Admin, for a maximum of 60 days per year.
