# Recruitment and Onboarding FAQ (Demo/Fictional)

Q: How do I get my employee ID card?
A: New employees receive their ID card from the HR desk within 5 working days of joining, after biometric registration.

Q: What documents are required at joining?
A: Original and photocopies of educational certificates, government-issued ID proof, address proof, and previous employment relieving letter (if applicable).

Q: How is probation handled?
A: New recruits serve a probation period of 2 years for Group A/B posts and 1 year for Group C posts. Confirmation is subject to satisfactory performance review and completion of mandatory induction training.

Q: Who do I contact for a transfer request?
A: Transfer requests must be submitted in writing to the Department Admin, who forwards eligible requests to the inter-department placement committee during the annual transfer window.

Q: Is there a dress code?
A: Employees are expected to wear formal or business-casual attire during office hours, except on designated ethnic-wear days.
