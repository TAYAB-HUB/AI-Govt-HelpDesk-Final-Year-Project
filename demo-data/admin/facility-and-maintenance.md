# Facility and Maintenance FAQ (Demo/Fictional)

Q: How do I report a maintenance issue (AC, electrical, plumbing)?
A: Raise a ticket under category "Administration - Maintenance" with the location and issue description; standard resolution time is 2 working days for non-urgent issues.

Q: What is the process for urgent facility issues (e.g., water leakage, power outage)?
A: Call the Administration helpdesk hotline immediately in addition to raising a ticket for priority dispatch.

Q: How do I request additional furniture?
A: Submit a furniture request ticket with justification; approval depends on the quarterly facilities budget and Department Admin sign-off.

Q: Are there designated smoking or break areas?
A: Yes, designated break areas are marked on each floor; smoking is only permitted in the outdoor zones as per building safety regulations.
