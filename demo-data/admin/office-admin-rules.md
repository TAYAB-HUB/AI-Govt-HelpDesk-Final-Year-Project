# Office Administration Rules (Demo/Fictional)

Office hours are 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday, with a 30-minute lunch break. Biometric attendance is mandatory at entry and exit.

Visitor management: all visitors must be registered at the reception desk with a valid ID; department staff must inform reception in advance for scheduled visitor meetings.

Conference room booking is done through the internal facilities portal on a first-come, first-served basis, with a maximum single booking of 3 hours.

Stationery and consumables are issued monthly per department quota through the Administration desk; additional requests need Department Admin approval.

Office space and seating allocation changes must be requested through the Administration ticket category and are reviewed during the quarterly space audit.
