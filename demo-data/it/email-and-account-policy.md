# Email and Account Policy (Demo/Fictional)

Every employee is issued an official email account on joining. Official communication must use the government email domain only; personal email accounts should not be used for office work.

Password requirements: minimum 10 characters, including uppercase, lowercase, a number, and a special character. Passwords must be changed every 90 days.

Mailbox storage quota is 5 GB per user. Employees nearing quota should archive old emails locally as per the IT retention guideline.

Shared department mailboxes (e.g., hr-helpdesk@, finance-helpdesk@) are managed by the respective Department Admin, who can add or remove delegate access.

Lost or stolen devices with access to official email must be reported to the IT helpdesk immediately so the account can be remotely locked.
