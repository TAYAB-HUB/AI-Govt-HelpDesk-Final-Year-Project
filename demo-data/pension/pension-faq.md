# Pension FAQ (Demo/Fictional)

Q: How do I check my pension application status?
A: Log in to the Pension Portal and check "Track My PPO" using your employee ID.

Q: What if my PPO is delayed beyond the expected date?
A: Raise a ticket under category "Pension - PPO Delay" with your employee ID and expected retirement date for escalation.

Q: Can I revise my nominee details after retirement?
A: Nominee/family pension details can be updated by submitting a fresh nomination form to the Pension Cell, countersigned by the pensioner.

Q: Is there a life certificate requirement?
A: Yes, pensioners must submit an annual life certificate every November, either physically at a designated office or digitally via the Jeevan Pramaan portal.

Q: How is pension revised after a Pay Commission update?
A: Pension is revised as per government orders issued after each Pay Commission; affected pensioners are notified via the portal and post.
