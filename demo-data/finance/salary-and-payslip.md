# Salary and Payslip FAQ (Demo/Fictional)

Q: When is salary credited?
A: Salary is credited to the registered bank account on the last working day of each month.

Q: How do I download my payslip?
A: Payslips are available under "My Payroll" in the employee portal from the 1st working day of the following month.

Q: What do I do if there is a salary discrepancy?
A: Raise a ticket under category "Finance - Payroll" with the specific month and discrepancy details; the Finance Officer will respond within 5 working days.

Q: How is income tax deducted?
A: Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) is calculated monthly based on the declared investment proofs submitted in the annual tax declaration window (typically April).

Q: Can I change my bank account for salary?
A: Submit a bank mandate change form with a cancelled cheque or bank passbook copy to the Finance desk; changes take effect from the next payroll cycle.
