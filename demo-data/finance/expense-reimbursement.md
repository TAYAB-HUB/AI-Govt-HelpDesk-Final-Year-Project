# Expense Reimbursement Rules (Demo/Fictional)

Employees can claim reimbursement for official travel, local conveyance, and approved training expenses through the Finance Reimbursement Portal.

Required documents: original bills/receipts, tour approval order (for travel), and the reimbursement claim form signed by the reporting officer.

Reimbursement timelines:
- Local conveyance claims: processed within 7 working days
- Travel/TA-DA claims: processed within 15 working days
- Training/course fee claims: processed within 20 working days

Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense being incurred; late claims require written justification and Department Admin approval.

Per-diem (daily allowance) rates for official travel depend on the employee's pay level and the city category (X/Y/Z) as per the latest Finance Department circular.
